# Rubber Stamp Travel Field Notes Prompt Template

每张照片单独填写并单独调用图片编辑工具。删除全部方括号变量，不要把说明文字渲染到成品中。

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: natural-realism rubber-stamp travel field-notes poster
Input image: this image is the sole reference and edit target. Do not use or combine any other uploaded photo.

Create one separate 4:3 landscape “Rubber Stamp Travel Field Notes Poster” from this photo. Never create a collage, diptych of multiple photos, contact sheet, or combined-location design.

OVERALL COMPOSITION
Use a natural visual transition between the original photograph on the left and an aged-paper field-note area on the right. Do not add an obvious dividing line. The left photograph occupies roughly 58% of the frame; the right paper area occupies roughly 42%.

LEFT — AUTHENTIC ORIGINAL PHOTOGRAPH
Keep the uploaded photograph visually authentic and recognizable. Preserve [subject], [perspective], [terrain or architecture], [plants or people], [spatial relationships], [natural light and shadows], [textures], and [overall atmosphere]. Do not redesign, reinterpret, move, replace, distort, or redraw the main subject.

Only apply a very subtle editorial treatment: gentle tonal balancing, restrained color grading, slightly softened highlights, and extremely fine natural film grain. It must still look like a real photograph taken on location rather than an AI-generated scene. Natural cropping is allowed only when needed for the 4:3 layout. Avoid excessive sharpness, HDR, artificial clarity, cinematic grading, or overly perfect detail.

RIGHT — AGED PAPER FIELD NOTES
Use warm off-white, slightly aged paper with believable physical texture: very subtle paper fibers, fine grain, faint handling marks, and a matte surface. Keep large areas completely unprinted. The paper must feel like a real sheet from an architect's travel notebook, not a designed poster background. Avoid strong stains, decorative textures, vintage filters, or artificial grunge.

SMALL HANDMADE RUBBER STAMP
Create one compact, imperfect, multi-color rubber stamp impression in the lower-middle portion of the right paper area, occupying approximately 30–38% of that area's height with generous blank paper around it.

Aggressively simplify the photograph into only these location-defining visual anchors: [anchor 1], [anchor 2], [anchor 3], [optional anchor 4], [optional anchor 5]. Preserve only their most meaningful silhouette, direction, scale, and spatial relationships. Remove crowds, cars, dense windows, repetitive buildings, tiny vegetation, decorative objects, and insignificant background details. The stamp must look like something a traveler could actually carve into a small rubber stamp, not a miniature illustration of the entire photograph.

Use only these 2–4 muted spot inks sampled naturally from the photo: [ink color 1], [ink color 2], [optional ink color 3], [optional ink color 4]. Each color is a separate physical ink layer. Add small believable handmade imperfections: slightly uneven pressure, tiny gaps in ink, dry areas, paper showing through, rough carved edges, irregular line thickness, small contour breaks, granular ink texture, faint ghosting, slight natural color-layer misregistration, and subtle edge variation. Avoid smooth vector edges, clean gradients, perfectly aligned digital layers, or exaggerated distress.

FIELD-NOTE TYPOGRAPHY
Place a small amount of quiet archival/typewriter text below or beside the stamp within the existing whitespace. Render exactly these four lines and no other text:
"LOCATION — [ENGLISH LOCATION NAME]"
"NO. — [NUMBER]"
"[KEYWORD 1] / [KEYWORD 2] / [KEYWORD 3]"
"[GREGORIAN YEAR]"

Spell every word correctly. Use understated typewriter or archival field-note typography with extremely slight mechanical irregularity. Keep it secondary to the photograph. Do not add slogans, brands, tourist phrases, decorative quotes, captions, or unnecessary text.

FINAL CHARACTER
The result should feel like a real travel photograph mounted beside a small handmade field stamp on an architect's personal notebook page: quiet, tactile, documentary, collectible, and genuinely handmade. The photograph remains the strongest visual element; the stamp is only a small fragment of memory extracted from it.

Avoid obvious dividing lines, circular seals, postage-stamp borders, perforations, wax seals, sticker layouts, souvenir-card designs, generic travel templates, smooth vector logos, polished digital illustrations, cartoon styling, 3D rendering, plastic textures, glossy gradients, excessive saturation, HDR, excessive text, decorative clutter, overly clean geometry, perfectly aligned ink layers, dense miniature architecture, and any alteration or redrawing of the original photograph.
```

## 字段示例

仅用于理解格式，不要照抄内容：

```text
LOCATION — SUMMER PALACE, BEIJING
NO. — 01
PAVILION / AUTUMN / LAKESIDE
2026
```
